Exploring how XR shapes the mind.

PhD candidate at the Computer Vision Center (Autonomous University of Barcelona) and research technician at the EventLab (Department of Neuroscience, University of Barcelona).

I work at the intersection of extended reality, neuroscience and human behaviour. At the Computer Vision Center and EventLab, I develop VR/XR experiences and experimental tools to study how immersive technologies influence perception, wellbeing and social decision-making. I also coordinate the Virtual Reality course in the Deep Tech Brain postgraduate program, where we connect XR with AI, neuroimaging and human perception.
