NEOBIC-XR: Home-Based Exercise in Oncology

Developed a home-based XR exercise platform for supportive oncology care, combining personalised avatars, self-observation, mixed reality, multilingual guidance and adjustable exercise intensity. The multicentre evaluation across three Spanish hospitals is ongoing. A 2026 methods paper describes the platform and evaluation framework.

VR Traffic Simulation for Social Awareness

Developed a VR traffic-safety and mobile-phone-awareness simulation in collaboration with the Servei Català de Trànsit. Participants experience a traffic incident from the victim’s perspective. Following an initial pilot with University of Barcelona students, the project is collecting data with participants in prison through the Department of Justice.

2023 – Present · EventLab
PRESENCE – EEG Hyperscanning for Co-Presence

Developed a multiplayer VR experience inspired by The Mind to investigate social co-presence and interpersonal synchrony through a silent cooperative task and EEG hyperscanning. Behavioural findings are being presented at ISMAR 2026; EEG data analysis is ongoing.

Preliminary EEG results and the post-processing pipeline were presented with Ana Zappa in an invited talk at CuttingGardens 2026.

2024 – Present · EventLab
ChronoXR — Timing for XR Experiments

Developed ChronoXR, an open-source Unity/OpenXR framework for stimulus and response timing in XR experiments. Combines frame prediction with asynchronous input acquisition to deliver corrected timestamps for behavioural measurements and physiological event markers. Validated at millisecond scale in standalone and Meta PC-VR configurations.

Presented with Michael Wiesing in an invited talk at CuttingGardens 2026.
